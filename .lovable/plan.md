
# 💕 Naveen's Proposal to Navina — Valentine's Day Website

A cinematic, elegant, and deeply romantic multi-page experience in red & dark tones that will take Navina on an emotional journey ending with your proposal.

---

## Page 1: The Mysterious Invitation (Landing Page)
- Dark red/black background with subtle floating rose petals animation
- A glowing envelope animation appears in the center
- Text fades in: *"Navina, someone has a secret for you..."*
- A beautiful "Open" button with a heartbeat pulse animation
- Soft particle effects (tiny hearts) floating across the screen

## Page 2: Floating Love Letters
- As she scrolls or clicks through, animated love letters float across the screen
- Each letter she clicks reveals a sweet message/reason why you love her
- Messages like: *"Your smile lights up my world"*, *"Every moment with you is magic"*, etc.
- Letters drift and sway gently with a dreamy red glow effect
- After reading several letters, a "Continue the journey..." button appears

## Page 3: Cinematic Love Story
- A dramatic, movie-style timeline with fade-in transitions
- Key moments presented like movie scenes with elegant typography
- Animated red roses blooming between each moment
- Smooth scroll-triggered animations — each section reveals as she scrolls
- Background transitions from dark to warm red tones as the story builds

## Page 4: The Countdown & Dramatic Reveal
- Screen goes dark with a single spotlight effect
- Dramatic text fades in: *"Navina..."*
- A cinematic countdown with heartbeat sound-like pulsing animation
- The screen fills with warm red light
- Beautiful calligraphy text appears: **"Will You Be Mine Forever?"**
- Two buttons appear with elegant animation: **"Yes 💕"** and **"Let me think... 🤔"**

## Page 5: The "Yes" Celebration 🎉
- When she clicks **Yes**: Hundreds of red hearts rain from the top of the screen
- A beautiful message appears: *"You just made me the happiest person alive, Navina"*
- Signed with love: *"Forever yours, Naveen ❤️"*
- Continuous heart rain animation with glowing effects
- If she clicks "Let me think" — the button playfully runs away from her cursor, making it impossible to click (she HAS to say Yes! 😄)

## Design & Visual Details
- **Color palette**: Deep reds, crimson, black, gold accents
- **Typography**: Elegant serif fonts for headings, soft sans-serif for body
- **Animations**: Rose petals falling, hearts floating, cinematic fade-ins, spotlight effects
- **Overall mood**: Luxurious, romantic, cinematic — like a love movie
